Cheap Cooker, Expensive Year: the Warranty Math Most Importers Skip

📅 Sep 9, 2026⏱ 2 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

The lower quote usually wins the PO. What the comparison skips is warranty cost: a cooker with a 1% failure rate and one with 5% can look identical on paper — and behave nothing alike in your first season. Every defective unit costs you a replacement, double freight, and an angry retailer.

Price the warranty before you sign

  • Warranty reserve = annual units × failure rate × (replacement unit + two-way freight + handling hours).
  • Add the reserve to each quote — compare true cost, not sticker cost.
  • A supplier with triple the failure rate is never the cheaper one.

Lock the failure terms in the PO

  • "Target defect rate ≤ 1%, verified by burn-in testing before shipment."
  • "Defect freight both ways paid by the supplier; replacement or credit within 15 days."
  • "Early-life failures (first 90 days in service) count as supplier defects."
