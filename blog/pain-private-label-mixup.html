Your Logo on the Wrong Box: the Private-Label Mix-Up That Sinks a Launch

📅 Sep 17, 2026⏱ 2 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

You launch your own cooker brand. The container arrives — and half the boxes show the wrong logo, two models are mixed in one carton, or the Russian/Arabic label is mistranslated. The buyer rejects the batch, your launch slips a season, and the factory shrugs. It's a private-label mix-up, and you paid for it.

Three clauses to lock in the PO

  • "Each model's logo, box art and label language are pre-approved by signed proof; no production without it."
  • "Cartons are scanned and verified per PO before sealing; mixed models in one carton are a reject."
  • "Factory bears the cost of any mislabeled or mixed batch — including rework and freight."

One gate before loading

Hold loading until a random carton check confirms logo, model and label match the signed proof. That ten-minute gate saves a season.

Send us your branding and model list — we'll lock label and box specs in the PO and run pre-loading verification so every unit ships correct.
