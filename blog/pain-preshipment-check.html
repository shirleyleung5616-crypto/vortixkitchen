Half Your Container Is Dead on Arrival - the 5-Point Check Before the Truck Leaves

📅 Aug 26, 2026⏱ 2 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

Dead-on-arrival (DOA) cookers are the costliest return: a 100% refund plus the freight back, and the buyer quietly switches supplier. Most DOA is caught - and fixed - at the factory, if you inspect before the container seals.

Write this 5-point check into your inspection order

  1. 100% power-on - every unit powers and heats, not a sample.
  2. Burn-in ≥ 30 min - IGBT and board fail under heat; a short test hides it.
  3. Glass and body - no crack, no scratch; carton drop-test record attached.
  4. Spec match - voltage, plug, label match the PO exactly.
  5. Third-party sign-off - independent inspector, report before you release the balance.

Release the last cent only after the report is clean.
