Pay a China Factory Safely — Even with Full Payment Before Shipment

📅 Sep 19, 2026⏱ 2 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

Vortix requires full payment before shipment — that is our standard. The way to make full upfront safe is not to hold a balance, but to lock the order in writing and verify the goods before they leave the factory.

What to put in the PO / contract

  1. Full payment before shipment — confirm the amount, our bank details and the loading date in writing first.
  2. Specify the goods exactly: model, power, plug, glass size, carton, and the inspection standard.
  3. Pay to a verified company account (TT), never a personal WeChat/Alipay.
  4. Verify the supplier — business license, export license, a live factory video call, and references from existing buyers.
  5. Arrange a third-party pre-shipment inspection (you order it, you pay) before the container loads, so you confirm the goods before they ship.

Full payment upfront is only risky with an unknown supplier. Lock the specs, verify the factory, and inspect before loading — then full payment is simply how the order runs.
