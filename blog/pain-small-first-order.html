Don't Bet a Container on a Supplier You Haven't Tested

📅 Sep 19, 2026⏱ 2 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

You pay for a full container, it ships, and the units sit — either they don't sell in your market, or a quality slip shows up after 2,000 units are on the water. Your cash is locked for a year and your shelf space is wasted.

Three lines to put in your PO

  • "First order capped at a trial quantity (e.g. 200-500 units); scale-up only after sell-through and a random QA pass."
  • "Factory provides a pre-shipment QA report on the trial batch; the full container is released only on pass."
  • "If trial-batch failure rate exceeds [X]%, refund or rework is the factory's cost, not yours."

One check before you commit

Run the trial through your own channel for 30 days. Real sell-through beats any factory promise — and it tells you the container is worth the money.
