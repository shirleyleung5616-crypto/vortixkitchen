Best Commercial Induction Cookers for Restaurants in 2026 (Buyer's Guide)

📅 Aug 12, 2026⏱ 5 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

Commercial kitchens are switching from gas to induction faster than ever — lower running cost, no open flame, and a cooler kitchen. But "commercial grade" means different things across suppliers. This guide shows what actually matters when you import induction cookers for restaurants, hotels and catering.

What "commercial grade" must deliver

  • Sustained high power: 3500W for countertop, up to 5000W+ for built-in wok stations — it must hold power under continuous service, not throttle after 10 minutes.
  • Thermal endurance: heavy-duty cooling (dual fan / high-CFM) and NTC thermal control so it survives a busy dinner service in a hot kitchen.
  • Build quality: reinforced glass, metal (not plastic) housing, and industrial-grade IGBTs rated for daily commercial load.
  • Safety: overheat, overvoltage and auto-shutoff protection are non-negotiable in a professional setting.

Choosing by venue

VenueRecommended specWhy
Small restaurant / café3500W countertop, single zoneCompact, fast, easy to place
Hotel / buffet4000–5000W built-in or twin zoneHigh throughput, looks integrated
Catering / outdoorPortable 2000–3500W with sturdy handleMovable, durable, simple power
Asian wok station5000W+ concave coil unitHigh, concentrated heat for stir-fry

Vortix commercial range (factory-direct)

Our T-series commercial induction line covers 3500W–5000W, with reinforced glass, metal housing and dual-fan cooling validated in chamber tests. Twin-burner (DCL series) and ultra-slim portable units are also available for mixed foodservice channels.

Tip for distributors: bundle a countertop 3500W unit with a twin-zone 5000W unit as a "starter kitchen set" — it's an easy up-sell to new restaurant clients.

Import checklist for commercial buyers

  1. Confirm continuous power rating, not just peak — ask for a 2-hour load test record.
  2. Match voltage to the destination market (220V vs 110V regions differ).
  3. Request spare-part list (fan, glass, IGBT) for after-sales.
  4. Clarify OEM options: logo, body color, power setting, packaging.
