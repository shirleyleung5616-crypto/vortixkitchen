Commercial vs Home Cooker: Pick Wrong and It Dies in 3 Months

📅 Sep 5, 2026⏱ 2 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

A restaurant buyer installs the cheap home unit you sold - and it dies in 3 months under continuous duty. You refund it, and lose the account.

Home units are rated for 1-2 hours/day. Commercial kitchens run 8-12. Same label, completely different life.

Put this in your PO

  • Commercial-grade: continuous-duty rated (tested 2-hr load), not peak-only.
  • IGBT with thermal margin; dual-fan + NTC at 40°C.
  • Reinforced glass + metal housing, not plastic.
  • Warranty: 12 months covering continuous commercial use - not "home use only" fine print.

Match the grade to the buyer: home resellers get home-grade; foodservice gets commercial. Right grade = fewer returns, real reorder.

Spec the right grade for your buyers?

Tell us your buyer type - retail or foodservice - and we'll spec the durability grade, thermal design and a commercial-covering warranty before you order.
