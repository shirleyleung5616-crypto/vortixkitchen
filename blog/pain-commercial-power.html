Wrong Wattage Cooker? The Power Mistake That Sends Restaurant Buyers Running

📅 Sep 5, 2026⏱ 2 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

Order the wrong wattage and the unit fails where it matters — or you over-pay for power the venue never uses. A 2000W unit on a wok station can't keep up at dinner rush; a 5000W unit in a small cafe trips the breaker and sits unsold.

Match power to venue before production

  • Cafe / light: 2000–3500W countertop.
  • Restaurant / buffet: 3500–5000W, twin-zone.
  • Wok / stir-fry: 5000W+ concave coil, boost mode.
  • Catering / street: 2000–3000W portable.

Put this in your PO

  • Wattage per model matched to the stated venue — not one size for all.
  • Circuit check: confirm the site's amp rating; 3500W+ needs a dedicated line or it trips.
  • Boost mode for peak loads.
  • Mixed-power bundle so one container covers cafe to wok.

Right power = no tripped breakers, no "can't keep up" complaints, real reorders.

Need the right power mix for your market?

Tell us your buyer mix — cafe, restaurant, wok — and we'll spec per-venue wattage, a circuit note, and a mixed bundle that fills one container.
