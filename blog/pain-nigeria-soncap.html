Your Container Is Stuck at Lagos Port — the SONCAP Step Most Importers Miss

📅 Aug 26, 2026⏱ 2 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

No SONCAP Certificate (SC) = no release at Lagos. Done after sailing instead of before = weeks of demurrage.

Steps

  1. PC — model-level, up to a year, after testing.
  2. SC — shipment-level, after pre-shipment inspection, before the container leaves China.

PO clause

"Supplier provides IEC 60335 reports, supports PC, coordinates SC inspection. SC number on all docs." A factory that can't name SGS/Intertek/BV hasn't shipped to Nigeria.
