Your Samples Pass, Your Bulk Fails — the BOM Swap You Never Signed

📅 Sep 7, 2026⏱ 2 min read✍️ Vortix Kitchen

Returns peak 2-3 months after launch. The cause is rarely bad luck — it's the factory swapping materials in mass production: copper coil to aluminum, thinner glass, cheaper cable, downgraded fan. The factory saves $1 a unit; you lose the returns, the reviews, and the brand.

Put three lines in your PO

  • "Key parts (coil, glass, cable, fan) brand & model locked in the BOM — no substitution."
  • "Golden sample sealed and signed by both parties; bulk must match it."
  • "Unilateral material change = breach; supplier reworks or refunds affected units."

On arrival, check in 10 minutes

  • Weigh the whole container against the bill of lading.
  • Open one unit and compare it to the sealed sample.
  • Photograph key parts as evidence before distribution.

Tired of good samples, bad bulk?

Send us your target market and we'll lock key components in the BOM, seal a signed golden sample, and put a no-substitution clause in your PO.
