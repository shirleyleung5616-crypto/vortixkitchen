Common Questions

Frequently Asked by Importers & Distributors

Straight answers to the questions buyers ask us most before placing an order.

What types of cookers does Vortix Kitchen manufacture?+

We produce a complete range of induction cookers (single and double burner), infrared ceramic cookers, ultra-slim portable models, and heavy-duty commercial-grade units. Power spans 1800W to 5000W across multiple series, covering home, retail, and restaurant applications.

Can you build cookers under our own brand (OEM / ODM)?+

Yes. We provide full private-label service: custom logo, packaging, body color, power specifications, and function settings. Our engineering team supports both OEM (your design) and ODM (our design, your brand) projects with low minimum order quantities.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?+

For standard models the MOQ starts from 100 units. For custom OEM / ODM projects the MOQ is confirmed case by case depending on tooling and printing requirements. Mixed models in one container are welcome.

Where is your factory, and can we visit it?+

Our factory is in Ronggui, Shunde, Foshan, Guangdong, China — the heart of the Chinese kitchen-appliance manufacturing cluster. Factory tours and video calls are welcome by appointment.

How long does production and delivery take?+

Standard orders usually ship within 15–30 days after deposit confirmation. Custom OEM / ODM projects typically need 30–45 days including sampling. We work with major freight forwarders for sea and air shipment worldwide.

Which markets do you export to?+

We have supplied 100+ global brands across Europe, North America, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Africa, and South America. Our products are built to meet the safety and quality standards required by major export markets.
